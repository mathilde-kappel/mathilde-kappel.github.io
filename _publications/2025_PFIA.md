**Abstract:** This research focuses on learning trajectory primitives for manipulating articulated objects in robotics. It aims to enable a robot to perform tasks autonomously in an open-ended environment. The method is based on learning a variety of expert trajectories that allow a robot to adapt its movements to the real environment.

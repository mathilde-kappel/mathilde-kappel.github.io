Welcome to my research page. This website gathers my publications, talks, projects, and ongoing work in robotics and intelligent manipulation.

I am a PhD student in robotic manipulation at [Sorbonne University](https://www.sorbonne-universite.fr/) and a member of the [ISIR laboratory](https://isir.upmc.fr/), where I work on learning robotic skills, quality-diversity methods, and sim-to-real transfer for manipulation.

Site overview
======
This site is organized around the main parts of my work:

- [Publications](/publications/) — papers and conference contributions
- [Talks and presentations](/talks/) — conferences, posters, and oral presentations
- [Portfolio](/portfolio/) — visual summaries and poster materials
- [Open Source Projects](/year-archive/) — project pages and code/dataset links
- [CV](/cv/) — full academic and professional background

I am particularly interested in robot learning, manipulation, and the design of data-driven methods that can transfer from simulation to real-world robotic systems.

Research environment
======
My work is carried out at [ISIR – Institut des Systèmes Intelligents et de Robotique](https://isir.upmc.fr/), a research unit in robotics and intelligent systems at Sorbonne University.
